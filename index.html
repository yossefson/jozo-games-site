In the studio.

Two digital puzzles in development.

Vane Chain

A chain of possibilities.

Arrange the vanes so that one rotation sets the next in motion. Build a chain that travels from the highest vane all the way down to the bell.

The puzzle is in the connections. Every piece has to reach the next.

Roundabout

Turn the world.
Find your way through.

Rotate the maze and guide a disc through its circular paths. Pushers and deflectors move it between rings, turning each rotation into a decision about where to go next.

Orbito

Four in a row.
Nothing stays put.

JoZo comes from the inventor of Orbito, the award-winning board game of shifting plans and sudden comebacks. You see your winning move. Your opponent sees theirs. One press of a button, and everything changes.
