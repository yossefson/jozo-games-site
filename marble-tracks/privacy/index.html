Marble Tracks Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026.

This policy covers the paid iPhone and iPad edition, the Android edition with real Google Play purchases, the browser edition and these game-specific information pages. Gameplay analytics applies only to Android releases that include this feature and only after you have accepted collection in the app. Older releases without analytics do not begin collecting data because this policy is updated.

Who operates Marble Tracks

Marble Tracks is operated by Yossef Sonnenfeld, an individual business operator based in Israel, trading as JoZo Games ("we" or "us"). We are responsible for the personal data processed for the game as described below.

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@jozo.games.

Information kept on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, unfinished placements, selected puzzle, tutorial progress, language, sound and music preferences, playback position and other game settings on your device or in your browser.

Your saved board layouts and exact piece placements stay on your device. In analytics-enabled Android releases, statistics about puzzle progress and hint use are sent as explained below. These statistics are not a backup of your saved game. The game has no player account or cloud save service.

The game checks device/browser language settings to adapt its language menu. On Android, it also checks whether a supported keyboard language is enabled. This check takes place locally; our game code does not send the keyboard-language results to us or Adapty and does not read what you type.

iPhone and iPad edition

Apple handles the one-time App Store purchase. This edition has no in-app purchases or advertising and does not use Adapty or send gameplay analytics. Progress, Daily Challenge results and preferences are stored on your device. You can play offline without a player account.

If you open a link to our website or contact us for support, the website and support sections of this policy apply. Apple processes App Store transactions and any diagnostic or usage information you choose to share with Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Gameplay analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase in supported Android releases to understand puzzle difficulty, how players progress and where the game can be improved. Collection is disabled until you accept it in the app; gameplay events from before acceptance are not queued for later upload.

The information includes:

  • Tutorial starts, completion and early exits where available.
  • Puzzles started and solved, hint use, the number of attempts and total puzzles solved.
  • The outcome of a marble run, such as reaching the goal, a collision, a fall, a loop, unused pieces, stopping the run or another interruption.
  • Active gameplay time, session activity and related event times.
  • Puzzle identifiers, puzzle and collection versions, difficulty, app version and interface language.
  • A Firebase app-instance identifier and technical information collected by the analytics service, such as device model, operating system, app information, sessions and approximate location derived from network information. We do not request GPS location.

The app-instance identifier allows activity from an installation to be associated over time. The data is pseudonymous, not completely anonymous. We do not send your name, email address, phone number, Support ID, exact board layouts or payment-card details in gameplay events. Advertising-ID collection is disabled, and our app does not grant analytics advertising-storage, advertising-user-data or personalized-advertising consent.

Google Play purchase and revenue information may also appear in Analytics through Google's store integration. Our current configuration does not forward Adapty profiles or Adapty purchase events to Firebase.

The startup privacy screen links to this policy before collection begins. Releases with the mandatory acknowledgement require you to select Accept before entering the game and do not provide an in-game analytics switch. Earlier releases may provide an optional collection choice and a switch in Settings. See Your choices and rights below for requests concerning collection and deletion.

The browser edition, installed browser edition and demo builds do not send gameplay analytics. Purchase-service processing is separate and can begin before the analytics acknowledgement, as described below.

Google processes analytics information on our behalf under the applicable Google Analytics data-processing terms. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy for more information about the provider.

Purchases and technical information

In the Android edition with real purchases, Google Play handles checkout. We use Adapty to verify purchases, manage access to paid puzzles and restore previously purchased access.

Adapty starts when the app opens, including when you have not made a purchase. It assigns a technical profile identifier without asking you to create a game account. These identifiers can distinguish installations and are not the same as completely anonymous information.

The purchase service processes:

  • Technical profile and device/app identifiers.
  • Device model, operating system, app/SDK versions and language-related metadata.
  • Installation and app-activity metadata, including last activity.
  • Purchase records and status, including the product, transaction references, purchase time, price/currency, access rights and refund or revocation information.
  • Events indicating that our purchase window was shown.

We use this information to operate purchases and restoration, keep paid access accurate, troubleshoot the purchase service and understand purchase-window and sales performance. We do not send your name, email or phone number as profile attributes. Our game does not ask for or store your payment-card number; Google handles payment details.

We configure Adapty to disable advertising-ID collection, IP-address collection/sharing and advertising attribution. These settings do not prevent network providers from processing the network addresses needed to deliver and secure a connection.

Adapty processes information on our behalf. See its end-user privacy policy. Google separately processes information for its store and payment services under its privacy policy.

The browser edition and demo builds with simulated purchases do not activate this Adapty purchase integration or take real payments.

Website and support

The browser edition uses local storage and cached game assets for saved settings and offline use. It requests game files, music and update information from our hosting provider, Netlify. Hosting infrastructure processes ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, requested resources and request times, to deliver and protect the website. These requests do not contain your puzzle progress.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. We use this information to answer you and handle support or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

These information pages on jozo.games are hosted on GitHub Pages, separately from the browser game. GitHub receives ordinary website request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure that hosting. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and do not receive your saved puzzle progress. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and disclosure

The game currently does not display advertising. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app targeted advertising.

We disclose information to service providers that operate analytics, purchases, hosting and email on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect users and address fraud or security incidents. We do not make players' information public.

Why we process information

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on fulfilling our agreement with you for requested purchases, restoration and support; legitimate interests for proportionate security, troubleshooting and understanding purchase-service performance; and legal obligations for records we are required to keep. Gameplay analytics is activated by your explicit acceptance in the app. Where we rely on consent, your rights concerning withdrawal are described below. Accepting this policy does not waive your statutory rights.

Purchase information is needed to verify and restore paid access. Technical profile collection in the real-purchase Android edition also occurs before purchase. There is currently no separate in-game switch for this collection.

Storage and international processing

We and our providers may process information outside your country, including in Israel and the United States. Where required, transfers use applicable contractual safeguards, including the standard contractual clauses available under the applicable Google Analytics and Adapty data-processing terms. You can contact us for information about relevant safeguards.

Local game data remains until it is changed or removed. Reset progress clears current progress and placements; it does not clear every setting or erase purchase records held by providers. Clearing app storage or browser site data removes the local data. Uninstalling does not itself request deletion from Adapty or Google.

We retain purchase-related records as needed to verify ongoing access, restore purchases and address refunds or disputes, and for any legally required period. We retain support messages as needed to resolve and document the request. Records that are no longer needed are deleted or de-identified, subject to necessary legal retention and providers' backup cycles.

Analytics user-level and event-level information is subject to the retention and deletion settings of our Google Analytics property. We retain analytics information to assess difficulty, progression and game performance over time, and review whether it is still needed for those purposes. Aggregated statistical reports may remain after individual event records expire. See Google's explanation of analytics retention. You can contact us about the applicable retention period or request deletion of identifiable records; deletion is subject to the identification limits explained below.

Security

We limit the information handled by the game: saved board layouts and exact placements remain on your device, while gameplay statistics are processed as described above. Payment-card details are handled by Google's checkout rather than collected or stored by our game. The purchase integration is configured to disable advertising-ID collection, IP-address collection/sharing and advertising attribution, as explained above.

Our purchase integration requests error-level SDK logging rather than verbose diagnostic logging. Temporary purchase diagnostic collection and export are disabled in production builds. The technical profile identifier is held in memory and shown under Settings > About to help with privacy requests; our game code does not add it to puzzle-progress saves or write it to its own purchase logs. The purchase SDK may separately retain data needed for its operation.

Local progress and settings are not given additional application-level encryption by our game. Their protection depends on the device, operating system and browser. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing sensitive information in support messages.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. If you suspect a security issue affecting your information, contact privacy@jozo.games without sending passwords or full payment-card details.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, obtain a copy, restrict processing or complain to your local data-protection authority.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal. Contact privacy@jozo.games for withdrawal requests. If your installed version offers an analytics switch, you can also disable collection there. In versions without that switch, stop using the native app to prevent further gameplay events while your request is being handled; the browser edition does not send gameplay analytics. Deleting past records does not itself prevent a device from sending new events if you continue using analytics-enabled features.

Send requests to privacy@jozo.games. In the Android edition, open Settings > About and find Support ID once the purchase service has loaded your technical profile. The screen shows an abbreviated identifier; the copy icon copies the full identifier. Include the copied identifier in your request to help us locate the Adapty purchase-service profile, even if you have not purchased. This Support ID is not your Firebase app-instance identifier and does not by itself identify your analytics records. If it is unavailable, you can still contact us. We may need limited additional information to verify your request. Do not send your full payment-card details. Data deletion is separate from a refund, and Google may retain its own records. Using the purchase service again can create a new technical profile.

For analytics requests, tell us that the request concerns gameplay analytics and which app version you use. We do not maintain a name or email directory of analytics installations. We may need the relevant installation identifier or other limited information to locate records and will explain what is needed. We cannot identify individual activity from an email address alone or remove a person's contribution from reports that no longer identify individuals. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove the installation identifier available on the device, but does not itself delete previously uploaded analytics records.

Younger players

Marble Tracks is designed for teenage and adult puzzle enthusiasts and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data practices change and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing materially different processing.
