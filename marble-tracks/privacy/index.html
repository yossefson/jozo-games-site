Marble Tracks Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026.

This policy covers the paid Android and Apple editions of Marble Tracks, the browser game and the game’s information pages. If you installed the earlier free Android edition, see the privacy notice for that edition.

Who operates Marble Tracks

Marble Tracks is operated by Yossef Sonnenfeld, an individual business operator based in Israel, trading as JoZo Games ("we" or "us"). We are responsible for the personal data processed for the game as described below.

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@jozo.games.

Information kept on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, unfinished placements, selected puzzle, tutorial progress, language, sound and music preferences, playback position and other game settings on your device or in your browser.

Your saved board layouts and exact piece placements stay on your device. You can play offline. The game has no player account or cloud save service.

The game checks your device or browser language settings to adapt its language menu. On Android, it also checks locally whether a supported keyboard language is enabled. It does not read what you type or transmit the result of this check.

Paid Android edition

You pay once on Google Play to download the full game. Google handles checkout under Google’s privacy policy. The game does not receive your payment-card details.

iPhone and iPad edition

You pay once on the App Store to download the full game. This edition sends no gameplay analytics. Puzzle progress, Daily Challenge results and preferences stay on your device. Apple handles checkout and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Gameplay analytics on Android

The paid Android edition uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand puzzle difficulty and how players progress. Collection starts only after you agree on the privacy screen in the game. Gameplay events from before that choice are not queued for later upload.

The following information is collected after consent.

  • Tutorial starts, completion and early exits where available.
  • Puzzles started and solved, hint use, the number of attempts and total puzzles solved.
  • The outcome of a marble run, such as reaching the goal, a collision, a fall, a loop, unused pieces, stopping the run or another interruption.
  • Active gameplay time, session activity and related event times.
  • Puzzle identifiers, puzzle and collection versions, difficulty, app version and interface language.
  • A Firebase app-instance identifier and technical information collected by the analytics service, such as device model, operating system, app information, sessions and approximate location derived from network information. We do not request GPS location.

The app-instance identifier allows activity from an installation to be associated over time. The data is pseudonymous. We do not send your name, email address, phone number, exact board layouts or payment-card details in gameplay events. Advertising-ID collection is disabled, and our app does not grant analytics advertising-storage, advertising-user-data or personalized-advertising consent.

The startup screen links to this policy. In the current paid Android edition, you must accept collection before entering the game. This edition has no in-game analytics switch. See “Your choices and rights” below to contact us about withdrawal or deletion.

The browser game, including when installed on your home screen, and demo builds do not send gameplay analytics.

Google processes analytics information on our behalf under the applicable Google Analytics data-processing terms. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy for more information about the provider.

Website and support

The browser edition uses local storage and cached game assets for saved settings and offline use. It requests game files, music and update information from our hosting provider, Netlify. Hosting infrastructure processes ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, requested resources and request times, to deliver and protect the website. These requests do not contain your puzzle progress.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. We use this information to answer you and handle support or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

These information pages on jozo.games are hosted on GitHub Pages, separately from the browser game. GitHub receives ordinary website request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure that hosting. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and do not receive your saved puzzle progress. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and disclosure

The game currently does not display advertising. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app targeted advertising.

We disclose information to service providers that operate analytics, hosting and email on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect users and address fraud or security incidents. We do not make players' information public.

Why we process information

We process support messages to respond to your requests. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on our legitimate interests for proportionate security and troubleshooting, and on legal obligations for records we must keep. Android gameplay analytics starts with your explicit acceptance in the app. Accepting this policy does not waive your statutory rights.

Storage and international processing

We and our providers may process information outside your country, including in Israel and the United States. Where required, transfers use applicable contractual safeguards, including the standard contractual clauses available under the applicable Google Analytics data-processing terms. You can contact us for information about relevant safeguards.

Local game data remains on your device until changed or removed. Resetting progress clears progress and placements. Clearing app storage or browser site data removes local data. This does not delete records already sent to Google Analytics.

We retain support messages as needed to resolve and document your requests. Records that are no longer needed are deleted or de-identified, subject to legal retention requirements and providers’ backup cycles.

Analytics user-level and event-level information is subject to the retention and deletion settings of our Google Analytics property. We retain analytics information to assess difficulty, progression and game performance over time, and review whether it is still needed for those purposes. Aggregated statistical reports may remain after individual event records expire. See Google's explanation of analytics retention. You can contact us about the applicable retention period or request deletion of identifiable records; deletion is subject to the identification limits explained below.

Security

Local progress and settings are not given additional application-level encryption by our game. Their protection depends on the device, operating system and browser. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing sensitive information in support messages.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. If you suspect a security issue affecting your information, contact privacy@jozo.games without sending passwords or full payment-card details.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, obtain a copy, restrict processing or complain to your local data-protection authority.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent to Android analytics or request deletion by emailing privacy@jozo.games. Withdrawal does not affect processing before withdrawal. In an edition without an analytics switch, stopping use of that edition prevents new gameplay events while we handle your request.

For analytics requests, tell us that the request concerns gameplay analytics and which app version you use. We do not maintain a name or email directory of analytics installations. We may need the relevant installation identifier or other limited information to locate records and will explain what is needed. We cannot identify individual activity from an email address alone or remove a person's contribution from reports that no longer identify individuals. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove the installation identifier available on the device, but does not itself delete previously uploaded analytics records.

Younger players

Marble Tracks is designed for teenage and adult puzzle enthusiasts and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data practices change and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing materially different processing.
