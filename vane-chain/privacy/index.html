Vane Chain Privacy Policy

Draft prepared 27 October 2026; revised 28 October 2026. Published for review only; not yet effective.

This draft covers the Android edition with real Google Play purchases, the browser edition and these game-specific information pages. An effective date will be added after the open decisions are resolved and the policy is approved for public launch.

Who operates Vane Chain

Vane Chain is operated by Yossef Sonnenfeld, an individual business operator based in Israel, trading as JoZo Games ("we" or "us"). We are responsible for the personal data processed for the game as described below.

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@jozo.games.

Information kept on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, unfinished placements, selected puzzle, tutorial progress, language, sound and music preferences, playback position and other game settings on your device or in your browser.

We do not upload your puzzle solutions, placements or hint activity. The game has no player account or cloud save service.

The game checks device/browser language settings to adapt its language menu. On Android, it also checks whether a supported keyboard language is enabled. This check takes place locally; our game code does not send the keyboard-language results to us or Adapty and does not read what you type.

Purchases and technical information

In the Android edition with real purchases, Google Play handles checkout. We use Adapty to verify purchases, manage access to paid puzzles and restore previously purchased access.

Adapty starts when the app opens, including when you have not made a purchase. It assigns a technical profile identifier without asking you to create a game account. These identifiers can distinguish installations and are not the same as completely anonymous information.

The purchase service processes:

  • Technical profile and device/app identifiers.
  • Device model, operating system, app/SDK versions and language-related metadata.
  • Installation and app-activity metadata, including last activity.
  • Purchase records and status, including the product, transaction references, purchase time, price/currency, access rights and refund or revocation information.
  • Events indicating that our purchase window was shown.

We use this information to operate purchases and restoration, keep paid access accurate, troubleshoot the purchase service and understand purchase-window and sales performance. We do not send your name, email or phone number as profile attributes. Our game does not ask for or store your payment-card number; Google handles payment details.

We configure Adapty to disable advertising-ID collection, IP-address collection/sharing and advertising attribution. These settings do not prevent network providers from processing the network addresses needed to deliver and secure a connection.

Adapty processes information on our behalf. See its end-user privacy policy. Google separately processes information for its store and payment services under its privacy policy.

The browser edition and demo builds with simulated purchases do not activate this Adapty purchase integration or take real payments.

Website and support

The browser edition uses local storage and cached game assets for saved settings and offline use. It requests game files, music and update information from our hosting provider, Netlify. Hosting infrastructure processes ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, requested resources and request times, to deliver and protect the website. These requests do not contain your puzzle progress.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. We use this information to answer you and handle support or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

These information pages on jozo.games are hosted on GitHub Pages, separately from the browser game. GitHub receives ordinary website request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure that hosting. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and do not receive your saved puzzle progress. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and disclosure

The game does not display advertising. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app targeted advertising.

We disclose information to service providers that operate purchases, hosting and email on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect users and address fraud or security incidents. We do not make players' information public.

Why we process information

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on fulfilling our agreement with you for requested purchases, restoration and support; legitimate interests for proportionate security, troubleshooting and understanding purchase-service performance; and legal obligations for records we are required to keep. If a particular activity requires consent, we will obtain it before that activity begins.

Purchase information is needed to verify and restore paid access. Technical profile collection in the real-purchase Android edition also occurs before purchase. There is currently no separate in-game switch for this collection.

Storage and international processing

We and our providers may process information outside your country, including in Israel and the United States. Where required, transfers use applicable contractual safeguards, including the standard contractual clauses provided under Adapty's processing agreement. You can contact us for information about relevant safeguards.

Local game data remains until it is changed or removed. Reset progress clears current progress and placements; it does not clear every setting or erase purchase records held by providers. Clearing app storage or browser site data removes the local data. Uninstalling does not itself request deletion from Adapty or Google.

We retain purchase-related records as needed to verify ongoing access, restore purchases and address refunds or disputes, and for any legally required period. We retain support messages as needed to resolve and document the request. Records that are no longer needed are deleted or de-identified, subject to necessary legal retention and providers' backup cycles.

Security

We limit the information handled by the game: puzzle progress and placements remain on your device, and payment-card details are handled by Google's checkout rather than collected or stored by our game. The purchase integration is configured to disable advertising-ID collection, IP-address collection/sharing and advertising attribution, as explained above.

Our purchase integration requests error-level SDK logging rather than verbose diagnostic logging. The technical profile identifier is held in memory and shown in Settings to help with privacy requests; our game code does not add it to puzzle-progress saves or write it to its own purchase logs. The purchase SDK may separately retain data needed for its operation.

Local progress and settings are not given additional application-level encryption by our game. Their protection depends on the device, operating system and browser. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing sensitive information in support messages.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. If you suspect a security issue affecting your information, contact privacy@jozo.games without sending passwords or full payment-card details.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, obtain a copy, restrict processing or complain to your local data-protection authority.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Send requests to privacy@jozo.games. In the Android edition, Settings displays a Privacy support ID once the purchase service has loaded your technical profile. You can copy this identifier into your request to help us locate the profile, even if you have not purchased. We may need limited additional information to verify your request. Do not send your full payment-card details. Data deletion is separate from a refund, and Google may retain its own records. Using the purchase service again can create a new technical profile.

Younger players

Vane Chain is designed for teenage and adult puzzle enthusiasts and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data practices change and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing materially different processing.
